For most adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes and adequate kidney function,
metformin is commonly recommended as initial pharmacologic therapy alongside
lifestyle changes, reflecting its established safety profile and favorable long-term
outcome data compared with early insulin initiation in this population.
