Metformin clearance depends on kidney function. When estimated glomerular filtration
rate (eGFR) falls into the moderately reduced range (commonly cited as roughly
30-59 mL/min/1.73m^2), many protocols call for dose reduction and closer monitoring
rather than first-choice use without adjustment, due to increased risk of drug
accumulation.
