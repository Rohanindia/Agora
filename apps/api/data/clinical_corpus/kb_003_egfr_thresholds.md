An eGFR around 45-59 mL/min/1.73m^2 is often categorized as moderately reduced kidney
function (commonly referenced as CKD stage 3a). At this stage, several reference
protocols recommend a clinician explicitly weigh metformin's benefits against
accumulation risk and consider dose reduction, rather than treating it as a default
choice.
